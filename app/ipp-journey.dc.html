Root cause

The product assumes (1) a self-service merchant with a payments team — many need more done for them, more reliably, with less to configure — and (2) a partner willing to hand-build around API, webhook and tooling gaps.

Both assumptions break at scale.

Insights, ranked (severity × frequency)

Every insight is tied to its evidence — volumes from Unwrap/Salesforce, plus a representative verbatim.

1Critical
Hardware & connectivity failures at the counter
Terminals that freeze, won't charge, or drop connectivity cost sales on the spot — the biggest pain by volume, including a physical battery-safety risk. The one bright spot: this theme is trending down.
Unwrap #1 (EFTPOS 26,369 = 42.6%) · ~9,100 reliability · leading NPS detractor
“An urgent safety issue with our three terminals… severe battery swelling, one unusable, another unable to charge.” — Salesforce ticket, Sep 2026
→ Stand up a named reliability + battery-safety workstream with merchant-visible device health.
2High
Terminal fulfilment: warehousing, logistics & orders (new)
Shipping delays, lost terminals and wrong items are a quarter of all IPP feedback with no improvement trend — but they hide in support tickets, so they barely show in NPS or Slack. A large operational drag with no clear product owner.
Unwrap #2 · W&L 15,778 (25.5%), flat · Orders & Returns 4,128
“Drop shipping is not working for Monzo… still an error saying drop shipping not configured.” — IPP support Slack
→ Treat fulfilment as a product surface: order/shipment tracking, lost-terminal self-service, fulfilment SLAs.
3High
Boarding, reassignment & provisioning
Terminals get stuck in ‘Deployed,’ reassignment isn't self-serve, and boarding silently fails when no payment method is set — some go live without encryption keys, and apps/memory can persist after reassignment (data-leak risk).
Boarding cluster ~7,500+ · keys-missing 1,825 · part-mapping 5,384 · methods not provisioned 4,288
“I am unable to access terminal S1F2… it is requesting a boarding password I cannot find how to change.” — Salesforce ticket, Sep 2026
→ Show boarding blockers in-product, enable self-serve reassignment with enforced data-wipe, auto-provision approved methods.
4High
Tap to Pay / SoftPOS is second-class and unreliable
Boarding and attestation fail too often (false ‘SecurityBlock,’ setup-token errors) and the tooling lags fixed terminals — device-level config is blocked, there's no phone-model targeting, and reboarding needs a reinstall.
~4,800+ entries, worst sentiment of any IPP theme · SDK failures 2,652 · Slack Integrations 26.3%
“Persistent HTTP 400 (1029_617) ‘Invalid combination of merchant account and setup token’ integrating Tap to Pay on Android.” — Salesforce ticket, Sep 2026
→ Bring SoftPOS to parity with terminals: reliable attestation/boarding, device-level config, phone-model targeting.
5High
Fleet & firmware updates are risky and uncontrollable
Saving a profile can push a fleet-wide install mid-trading, upgrades sometimes uninstall apps, and recovery is undocumented. Partners want the missing controls to validate before going wide.
Lightspeed · IPPMOB-7350 · IPPPL-15999
“My terminal is stuck at 71% during a software update, showing the wrong timezone and ‘terminal not boarded.’” — Salesforce ticket, Sep 2026
→ API-driven deployment, staged rollout, and stable LTS tracks.
6High
Developer & platform / API integration overhead
Partners who embed Adyen hit heavy integration overhead: API specs that don't match production, 7–9 onboarding calls where Stripe needs 0, no bulk payment-method endpoints, missing fields (MCC, midNumber), missing offline webhooks, and no real-time payout API.
Lightspeed developer feedback
“Integrating with Adyen creates disproportionate engineering overhead… critical API gaps, inaccurate specs, unusable log-tracing, poor device deployment controls.” — Lightspeed
→ Close the API/tooling gaps before strategic partners benchmark toward competitors.
7High
Settings & Customer Area are opaque — and the UI regressed
~955 terminal settings, each settable at six cascading levels (PSP → Company → Merchant → Store → Profile → Terminal), yet nothing is labeled device-only — so merchants can't tell what a setting is, where it came from, or what else changes. The new ‘Ekiben’ UI also broke working controls.
IPPPL-17966 · PF-4562 · Settings PRD, Unwrap, Lightspeed
“Too many settings are invisible to the customer and can only be viewed by Adyen support… you don't know what you don't know.” — NPS, Dick's Sporting Goods (2/10)
→ Make settings legible: show current value + provenance + blast radius, and label device-only settings.
8Medium
Thin docs, slow support, low self-serve
Merchants can't self-serve: docs lack detail and functionality often only works after a conversation with support. Support and Reporting are IPP's lowest-rated NPS dimensions.
NPS H1 2026 · lowest-rated dimensions
“Documentation often lacks and often when we try to implement functionality it does not work unless we have a conversation with support.” — NPS, Quilt Software (8/10)
→ Invest in docs + self-serve so common tasks don't require support.
9Medium-High
Payments configuration & behavior (US debit/PIN, limits, schemes)
Config behaviour surprises merchants — e.g. debit cards running as credit, or PIN not prompting — smaller by ticket volume but high-severity at the counter.
~1,600 Salesforce tickets · recurring in Slack
“The POS is running debit cards as credit, and I need the setting enabled so debit requires a PIN.” — Salesforce ticket, Jan 2026
→ Make payment-config outcomes predictable and self-serviceable.
10Medium
Localization / regional fit blocks markets
Regional gaps (Japan, France/Conecs, receipts, language) can gate whole markets for local teams and merchants.
NPS H1 2026 · regional detractors/passives
“The Adyen Japan team is struggling to serve their Japanese customers.” — NPS, Dinii Inc. (Japan, 8/10)
→ Prioritise regional fit where it unblocks whole markets.
11Medium
Store & Forward / offline is inconsistent
Offline / Store & Forward behaves unexpectedly on unstable networks — including double payments — eroding trust exactly when reliability matters most.
NPS H1 2026 · EMEA
“Offline payments… we often experience double payments in these situations.” — NPS, Georg Jensen A/S (10/10)
→ Make offline payments deterministic (no double charges/declines).
12Medium
No business-value / reporting layer on fleet data
Merchants want actionable reporting from device/fleet data (e.g. per-device feature status) and today have to ask support for it.
Salesforce tickets · fleet reporting requests
“I need a report showing the Standalone status for every V400m device in our stores.” — Salesforce ticket, Sep 2026
→ Add a reporting layer that turns fleet data into merchant-visible insight.

Blind spot 1 — Fulfilment has no owner

Warehousing & logistics is the 2nd-largest pain (25.5% of IPP feedback) yet was never on the roadmap list — it hides in support tickets, not NPS or Slack.

Blind spot 2 — Partners benchmark us against Stripe

Platform partners such as Lightspeed carry enough API/tooling overhead to compare Adyen unfavorably against Stripe — a strategic churn risk if left unfixed.

System context & documentation

Technical architecture, configuration hierarchy, and documentation references gathered from Hub and Adyen Docs — grounding the journey map in how the system actually works.

Supply chain journey

The IPP Platform Supply Chain team smooths the terminal's journey from warehouse to merchant:

  • •Dropshipping — direct from warehouse to submerchant
  • •Custom packaging — leaflets, QR codes, branded boxes for platform merchants
  • •Bulk shipping — safety stock (max 6 months before firmware staleness)

Terminal boarding journey

The boarding sequence, traceable via log queries:

listStores→ getConfiguration→ selfBoarding→ merchant.json→ confirm→ maintenance

Maps to journey steps 2.c → 3.c: the terminal receives its identity, downloads config, and enters operational state.

Configuration journey

Settings inherit downward through 5 levels; overrides propagate to children unless overridden further down.

PSP → Company → Merchant → Store → Terminal

73% of settings are overridden at the terminal level, with no single entry point for the settings application.

TFM data model: tfmconfig, tfmterminal, store, account, tfmprofile. EMV kernel config runs a 5-phase flow on boot or settings change.

AfP terminal onboarding

"Onboarding the terminal can be a source of friction and therefore impact the extent to which users fully adopt the solution. If the setup process is too difficult, users may drop off before they have fully adopted terminal payments."

— Adyen Docs, Platforms Quickstart Guide

— Adyen Docs, Platforms Quickstart Guide

Recommendations from docs (for AfP platforms)

  • 01Automate onboarding early. Start with operational support but focus on automating through a 'Terminal Manager' in the platform's customer environment — more scalable, better UX.
  • 02Define adoption benchmarks. Determine when a terminal is considered 'adopted' to monitor performance and identify improvement areas.
  • 03Maintain healthy safety stock. Low inventory risks out-of-stock; terminals in warehouse >6 months get stale firmware & low battery.
  • 04Consider software-first. Tap to Pay / Mobile SDK provides an easier starting point — less logistics, lower upfront investment. Move to hardware as users mature.
